# MOOFI - Habit & Wellness Tracker 🌿

**MOOFI** is an Android app built with **Kotlin** that helps users cultivate healthy habits, track daily routines, and understand emotional patterns. By combining habit tracking, mood journaling, and smart reminders, MOOFI makes wellness effortless and motivating.  

---

## 🎯 App Idea
Develop consistent healthy habits and monitor emotional wellbeing. Automate reminders for tasks like drinking water or meditating, and visually track progress to stay motivated.  

---

## ✨ Core Features
- **Daily Habit Tracker**: Add, edit, delete habits and see daily completion progress  
- **Mood Journal**: Log moods with emojis, date/time, and view past entries in a list or calendar  
- **Hydration Reminder**: Notifications to drink water at chosen intervals  
- **Advanced Feature**:  
  - Home-screen Widget showing today’s habit completion    

---

## 🛠 Technical Details
- **Architecture**: Activities & Fragments for modular screens (Habits, Mood Journal, Settings)  
- **Data Persistence**: SharedPreferences for storing user data  
- **Intents**: Explicit & implicit intents for navigation and sharing summaries  
- **State Management**: Retains user settings across sessions  
- **Responsive UI**: Works on phones & tablets, in portrait & landscape  

---

## 💡 Creativity
- Combines habit tracking and mood journaling to identify patterns in wellbeing  
- Automated reminders reduce mental load  
- Visual progress and charts motivate long-term habit adherence  

---

**MOOFI** helps users build better habits, understand their emotions, and improve overall wellness—all through a simple, intuitive Android interface.
